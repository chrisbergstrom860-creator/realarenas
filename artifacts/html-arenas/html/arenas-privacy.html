Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, share, and protect your information when you use Arenas.

Last updated: September 4, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Muscletowel LLC, a California limited liability company ("Arenas," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use the Arenas website, applications, and services (the "Service").

By using Arenas, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service. This policy is incorporated into and works alongside our Terms of Service.

2. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

Information you provide directly:

  • Account information: email address, display name, username/handle, and password (stored securely in hashed form by our authentication provider).
  • Profile information: city or general location, sports you participate in, bio, and any other details you choose to add.
  • Activity data: workouts and training you log, including sport, distance, duration, dates, "feeling," and related notes.
  • Social and community content: posts, comments, club memberships, club information you create or manage, follows, invitations, event RSVPs, and challenge participation.
  • Communications: messages you send to us (for example, through our contact form).

Information collected automatically:

  • Usage data: how you interact with the Service — pages viewed, features used, and actions taken.
  • Device and technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, and similar technical identifiers.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: see Section 6.

Information from third parties:

  • If we later offer connections to external services (for example, device or activity-sync integrations such as Strava), we would receive data from those services only with your authorization, and this policy would be updated to describe it.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information beyond what is inherent in fitness activity data. Please do not share special-category data (such as detailed health conditions) that you do not want associated with your account.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account and provide the Service;
  • Display your activities, posts, profile, and community participation to you and to other members as described in Section 4;
  • Provide club-management features, including training summaries, reports, inactivity monitoring, and check-in messages;
  • Calculate leaderboards, rankings, streaks, challenges, and similar features;
  • Communicate with you about your account, security, and important Service updates (transactional communications);
  • Respond to your support requests;
  • Maintain the security, integrity, and proper functioning of the Service, and detect and prevent fraud or abuse;
  • Improve and develop the Service;
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as described below:

  • With other users: Your profile, posts, and community participation may be visible to other members according to the Service's design. Think before you post.
  • With club administrators and coaches: Club administrators and coaches can see the activity you log while you are a member of their club. On Club Pro clubs, this includes weekly training hours, trends against your recent average, sessions, distance, rest days, and periods of inactivity, shown alongside your name, unless you turn off Club training analytics in Settings. Managers may be shown classifications such as training above or below your usual level, and may send you check-in messages based on them.
  • Your leaderboard and activity-feed settings do not limit what club managers can see. The Club training analytics setting does. The "Show on leaderboards" and "Activity feed visible" settings limit where your activity appears for other members and followers. Turn off "Club training analytics" to stop named individual training analysis and training check-ins; leaderboards, club totals, and club membership are unaffected.
  • With service providers: We use trusted third parties to operate the Service. These currently include or are expected to include:
    • Supabase — authentication and database hosting;
    • Railway — application hosting;
    • Resend — transactional email delivery for account notices, club invitations, contact-form messages, and subscription confirmations. Depending on the message, Resend receives the recipient’s email address, subject, and email content; contact-form messages also include the sender’s email as the reply-to address.
    • Stripe — payment processing and subscription billing. Arenas provides Stripe with your email address, selected plan, and internal account or club identifiers. Stripe may collect your name, billing information, and payment details directly through its hosted checkout and stores that payment information; Arenas stores subscription identifiers and status but not card or bank details.
    These providers process data only on our instructions and as needed to provide their services.
  • For legal reasons: We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Arenas, our users, or others.
  • In a business transfer: If Arenas is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

5. AI Insights

If you subscribe to Individual Pro and use AI Insights, Arenas sends a summary of your own training data to Anthropic, the provider of the Claude AI model, to generate answers to your questions. The summary includes activity counts, durations, distances, points, sport breakdowns, streaks, personal records, time-period totals, and your own standing on Arenas leaderboards (your rank, the number of ranked athletes, your points, and your activity count; for club leaderboards, also the club name and your role in it). It does not include your name, email, user ID, activity titles or notes, individual activity records, or any other athlete's data.

If you use training plans, events, or goals, the summary also includes your upcoming planned sessions (date, sport, title, planned duration, and status), events you've RSVP'd to or that belong to clubs you're a member of (date, title, sport, type, club name, and your own RSVP), a monthly count of past planned sessions marked done, skipped, or still planned, and your active goals (type, sport, target, period, progress, whether each is complete and on track, and the dates of its current period). It does not include session notes, event descriptions or locations, other attendees, or other members' RSVPs.

The summary also includes how many items of each kind were sent and whether any were left out because of a limit.

Your question is sent with the summary, along with up to three of your previous questions and answers from the same session so the conversation can continue. Anthropic processes this data to generate a response and handles it under its own terms and privacy policy.

Arenas keeps a count of how many questions you've asked each month. It does not store your questions or the answers.

AI Insights is only used when you ask it a question. No training data is sent to Anthropic otherwise.

6. Data retention

We keep your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we need to retain certain data to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, or maintain security (and except for residual copies in routine backups for a limited time).

Some content you shared in community spaces (for example, comments or club activity others have interacted with) may persist after account deletion in a de-identified or aggregated form.

7. Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you logged in, remember your preferences, and operate the Service.

We use Plausible, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to understand overall usage of the Service — things like which pages are visited and how often. Plausible is cookieless and does not collect or store any personal data: it sets no cookies, records no personal identifiers, and does not track you individually across sites.

If you are in the EU or UK, you have rights under the GDPR, including the rights listed above and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.
