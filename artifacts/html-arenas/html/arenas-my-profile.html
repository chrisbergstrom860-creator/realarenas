Delete account
This is permanent. There is no undo.
Deleting your account removes, permanently:
  • Your profile, photo, preferences and settings
  • All activities, goals, planned sessions and achievements
  • Your posts, comments and likes
  • Your followers and everyone you follow
  • Event RSVPs and challenge entries — plus any events and challenges you created
  • Club memberships and invites, and any club where you're the only member
  • Any paid subscription is canceled first — you won't be billed again
If you're the only admin of a club that still has members, deletion is blocked until you hand over the admin role or delete the club.
